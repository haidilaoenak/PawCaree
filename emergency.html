🩹 First Aid

General Emergency Steps

Essential steps to take before contacting a veterinarian.

😌

1. Stay Calm

Your pet senses your anxiety. Being calm helps you think clearly and act effectively.

🔍

2. Assess Condition

Is breathing normal? Any visible wounds? Can they stand? Identify the key symptoms first.

📞

3. Call a Vet

Contact the nearest vet clinic immediately. Describe symptoms clearly and calmly.

🚗

4. Transport Safely

Wrap your pet in a towel. Keep them warm and as still as possible during transport.
