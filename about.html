👋 Our Story

About PawCare

Built with love by pet lovers, for every pet owner in Indonesia and beyond.

💙 Our Mission

Every Pet Deserves the Best Care

PawCare was born from the belief that caring for a pet should be easy, joyful, and full of love — not confusing or stressful.

We bring together everything you need in one platform: from everyday health information to fast emergency access, grooming, and a curated pet product marketplace.

PawCare is a prototype platform built as a demonstration of an integrated pet care service concept. We believe every small detail in your pet's life matters — and we're here to help you manage all of it with ease.
